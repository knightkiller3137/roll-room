How Ludo works here

Race all four of your tokens around the board, up your own home lane, and into the center. This room uses one clear ruleset based on the standard Ludo rules and the marked safe cells on the supplied board.

  1. Get a token out: Roll a 6 to move a token from base onto your color’s arrow-marked start square. A 6 gives another roll.
  2. Move and capture: Move one token clockwise by the rolled number. Landing on an opponent sends them back to base. A same-color pair blocks opponents from passing or landing there.
  3. Safe cells: Your own start square and the four starred cells are safe. Opponents cannot capture a token on a safe cell.
  4. Reach home: After one full lap, enter your color’s home lane. You need an exact roll to reach the center. First player with all four tokens home wins.
  5. Three sixes: A third consecutive 6 ends that turn. A roll with no legal move passes automatically. Captures do not award an extra roll in this version.

Fair-play dice

Classic: every roll is uniformly random from 1 to 6, generated by the server. Gentle start: all players receive a 6 after three of their own turns if all four tokens remain in base. This is an optional, visible house rule—not hidden dice weighting. The server must enforce it.

Rule references: Masters Traditional Games and Yellow Mountain Imports. Their rules agree on 6-to-enter, extra roll for a 6, captures, blockades, and exact home entry; specific safe-cell and repeated-six conventions can vary by set.

For a fair online match, the client never rolls the die or decides a legal move. Those decisions belong to the room server.
